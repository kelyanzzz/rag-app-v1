# Nimbus Billing and Plans

## Plans

Nimbus has three plans. The Free plan includes 10 GB of storage and 1 GB of egress per month, for one user. The Team plan costs 12 euros per user per month, with 2 TB of pooled storage and 500 GB of egress per month. The Enterprise plan is priced by contract and includes a 99.99% availability SLA, single sign-on with SAML, and a dedicated support manager.

## Overage

On the Team plan, storage above the pooled 2 TB is billed at 0.018 euros per GB per month, and egress above 500 GB is billed at 0.05 euros per GB. Free accounts are never billed for overage: writes are blocked instead when the quota is reached.

## Invoices and payment

Invoices are issued on the first day of each month and paid by card or by SEPA direct debit. If a payment fails, Nimbus retries on day 3, day 7 and day 14. After the third failed attempt the account is switched to read-only until the invoice is paid.

## Refunds

Annual Team subscriptions can be refunded in full within 14 days of purchase. After 14 days, no partial refunds are given for the remaining months, but the subscription can be cancelled so that it does not renew. Monthly subscriptions are never refunded. Overage charges are never refunded, except when the overage was caused by an incident on the Nimbus side, which the support team confirms.

## Changing plan

Upgrading from Free to Team takes effect immediately. Downgrading from Team to Free takes effect at the end of the billing period, and only if the stored data is below 10 GB on that date; otherwise the downgrade is cancelled and the owner is notified.

## Discounts

Registered non-profit organisations and schools receive 40% off the Team plan. Students can use the Team plan for free for 12 months with a valid university email address.
